Our story

Why we only make one cookie

Most bakeries do a little of everything. At Krivian Bakery we picked the cookie everyone loves and made it bigger, thicker and better.

Every cookie is a full 6 oz, shaped tall so it bakes up round and domed instead of spreading flat. You get a crisp outside, a soft center and chocolate in every bite.
